    04Why work with me

    Software is just one arrow in the quiver

    Alex Steingart

    Hospitality strategist · Founder, Par/Ex Analytics · Ex-VP Engineering, Provi · CTO, SevenFifty · Cornell MMH ’26

    I spent twenty years building business workflow software, the last eleven for hospitality supply chains. As CTO of SevenFifty, I built tools across the beverage alcohol three-tier system: retailers cut inventory and procurement time by 50%, and distributor sales reps grew book volume 20% with tools built around how they actually sell. At Provi, I led the 60+ person engineering org. The best of that work started by modeling the whole job to be done, then handing software only what it does better than people, so skilled staff could spend their time on guests and customers.

    But I kept meeting challenges where more software would only distract from the guest. All I had was a hammer, and not every challenge was a nail. So I came to Cornell's Master of Management in Hospitality to expand my toolset. In summer 2026, working the floor at a busy upscale restaurant, I put it to use: aligning role design with incentives grew covers 10% on sold-out nights, and I standardized beverage recipe costing across three units.

    Par/Ex is my independent practice. I diagnose, design, and implement every engagement myself.
